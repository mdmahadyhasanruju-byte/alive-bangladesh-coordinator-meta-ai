# Privacy, Consent, and Retention Plan

This is a proposed baseline for review, not legal advice or proof of compliance. Obtain locally appropriate review before collecting real participant data.

## Consent form draft

> **About this activity:** We are testing a tool that turns community feedback into a draft for a human coordinator to review. Participation is voluntary. You may decline recording, skip a question, or stop at any time without losing access to services.
>
> **What may be collected:** Your answers and, only if you separately agree, an audio recording and/or photograph. We will avoid collecting names and precise locations unless they are necessary, safe, and separately explained. Do not share information about another person without their permission.
>
> **How it is used:** Authorized project staff and approved processing services may use the information to prepare a draft. AI output can be wrong. A human must check and approve any record before it is treated as an action or commitment.
>
> **Storage and deletion:** For the public demo, synthetic data only. Demo records are scheduled for deletion within 30 days. For any later real-data pilot, the retention period, access roles, deletion route, and service providers must be disclosed before collection.
>
> **Your choice:** I understand this activity and agree to the options I selected: text processing [ ]; audio recording and processing [ ]; photograph processing [ ]. I know how to stop or request deletion. Date/time: ______. Consent recorded by: ______.

## Required controls

- Public demonstrations use synthetic data only; do not put real participant names, contact details, faces, precise locations, or private stories in demo fixtures.
- Require separate, informed choices for text, audio, and photographs. Consent is revocable for future processing.
- Set demo retention to 30 days, with a defined deletion job and a way to handle deletion requests. Verify deletion from primary storage and applicable derived copies before claiming completion.
- Minimize collection, access, and location precision. Store only what the agreed purpose requires.
- Do not log secrets, raw audio, photographs, transcripts, or direct identifiers. Logs should contain minimal operational metadata and a safe opaque request identifier.
- Encrypt data in transit and at rest when real data is authorized; define role-based access and incident handling before pilot use.
- Require human approval before any extracted record is used for decisions, commitments, referral, publication, or resource allocation.
- Keep an auditable record of consent state, reviewer action, correction, and deletion without retaining unnecessary source content.

## Before any real-data use

Complete a privacy and safety review, identify the responsible data controller and processors, check applicable local requirements, assess language-specific consent comprehension, document access and deletion behavior, and test the controls. Until then, status is **NOT VERIFIED / synthetic demo only**.
